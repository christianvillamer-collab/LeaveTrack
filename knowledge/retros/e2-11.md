# E2-11 retro — HR over-balance override

## What landed

- Migration `0007_hr_over_balance.sql` adds `hr_override_approved`/`hr_override_rejected` to the
  `audit_log.action` check and allows balances down to `-10000`.
- `applyDecision` has a single over-balance switch: managers keep the 409, HR overrides record the
  true negative `previous - days` value. Both modes still share the lock, pending/duplicate guard,
  balance update, status update, audit insert, and notification outbox transaction.
- Override audit rows are now filterable by their distinct action; the detail keeps the
  `HR override` prefix and labels the over-balance transition.
- The manager over-balance regression test, HR over-balance success test, and migration assertions
  cover the acceptance criteria.

## Decisions

- **No silent clamping.** A short HR approval writes the real negative balance. The `-10000` floor
  is a safety rail, not a clamp.
- **One decision path.** The flag is passed by the caller instead of forking the transaction. The
  manager gate remains the default; only `overrideLeaveRequest` opts in.
- **Distinct audit actions.** Reports no longer need to parse the detail prefix to tell an override
  from a normal decision, while the prefix is retained for older reports and readability.
